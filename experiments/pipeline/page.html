Who may send data?

The list of approved gateways is stored on the blockchain and checked by a smart contract for every batch. A gateway that is not on the list is refused: its data never reaches the chain.

Is the stored data safe?

A batch is first "settling": it is in a block, but a rare rollback could still move it. After 60 more blocks it is final: it can no longer be changed or removed by anyone.
