# Review of the web page: wording and usability problems for a first-time viewer

File reviewed: [`experiments/pipeline/page.html`](../experiments/pipeline/page.html) (HTML and JavaScript read; the page was not run). Nothing is changed; each item has a proposed fix. Most important first. Line numbers refer to `page.html`.

1. **"Readings stored permanently" shows 0 for the first 5 minutes** (lines 103, 225). It counts only final batches, but the Sensors tab already shows readings that are on the chain. A viewer sees values moving and "0 stored permanently" and thinks something is broken. The page does not say why. *Fix:* rename to "Readings final (60 blocks deep)", add a small second line "readings in a block, not yet final: N", and a tooltip "a batch becomes final about 5 minutes after it is written".

2. **Two sentences say more than the system guarantees** (lines 140-141 and 310-311). "Final: it can no longer be changed or removed by anyone" is true only on this private chain with 3 pools, with k = 60 ([`demo README`](../experiments/demo/README.md), Limits). The Verify text "These N readings were written to the blockchain by an approved gateway" uses the present tense of a status that may have changed, and a viewer can read it as "the readings are correct". *Fix:* "Final: more than 60 blocks deep; on this test chain it can no longer be rolled back" and "These N readings were written by gateway X, which was on the approved list when the batch was written. This does not show that the sensors measured correctly."

3. **Nothing on the page says the data is simulated and the chain is a private test chain** (title and header, lines 6, 101). A new viewer may take "Sensor data on the blockchain" for real field data. *Fix:* a permanent small label in the header: "Test network, simulated sensors".

4. **A revoked gateway looks contradictory** (line 286). In demo step 4 the row says "Not approved, its data is refused" next to "Batches stored 12". The batches stay on the chain; only new data is refused. *Fix:* "Not approved now: new data is refused. Earlier batches stay on the chain."

5. **The opened proof can vanish, and rows move under the mouse** (lines 298, 475-480). Only the 8 newest batches are listed; a batch arrives every 10 s in the demo, so a batch whose proof is open leaves the list after about 80 s. New rows also push the Verify buttons down every 10 s, so a click can hit the wrong row. *Fix:* keep opened proofs even when the batch is older than the 8 listed (or list 20), and add new rows without redrawing the rows the viewer is using.

6. **"Approved gateways 1 of 2" is unclear** (lines 104, 226-228). "Of 2" means "of the gateways the page knows by name", not all gateways; and "-" appears with no reason when the list cannot be read. *Fix:* "1 approved, 1 not approved" or "1 of 2 known gateways"; for "-" show "list not readable" with a tooltip.

7. **"Settling 12 / 60" has no unit and no time** (line 281). *Fix:* "Settling: 12 of 60 blocks (about 4 min to go)". The text above the table says "60 more blocks" but never says this is about 5 minutes.

8. **Too many unexplained technical words.** "Settling", "rolled back", "receipt no.", "Depth", "Indexer (Kupo)", "slot", "tip", "signer", "unknown signer", "list unknown" (lines 143-145, 281, 310, 321-322). The "rolled back" counter (line 145) has no explanation at all on the page (the tooltip is only on the badge, line 278). *Fix:* one line under each heading in plain words, for example "Rolled back: removed after a chain reorganisation; should stay 0". Keep technical words on the Details tab only, or add a short legend.

9. **Times have no time zone and mix three clocks** (lines 196-200, 403, 428). Axis labels show HH:MM:SS with no zone; the tooltip says "UTC (sensor clock)"; "x s ago" is the server clock; "Server time (UTC)" is on the Details tab. The audience sits in a local time zone. *Fix:* write "UTC" on the axis, and add one sentence on the Sensors tab: "Times are UTC. 'Ago' is measured by the page server."

10. **The graph is hard to find and its link to the cards is weak** (lines 117-124, 468-474). The graph is at the top, while the sentence "Click a sensor to see its graph" is small grey text below it. A graph already shows before anyone clicks, so it is not clear that it can be changed. With many cards (for example 30), a click far down changes a graph that is out of view. *Fix:* put "Click a card to show its graph" in the graph header, scroll the graph into view or make it sticky, and mark the selected card more clearly.

11. **The graph's axes and limit lines are not explained** (lines 387, 396-400). Points are spaced evenly in reading order, not by time, so a silent period is not visible as a gap; the time labels are therefore unevenly spaced. The red dashed limit lines have no label. The note says only "in the order they were measured" (line 377). *Fix:* write "points are equally spaced, not to time scale" in the note, label the dashed lines "limit 60" or "limit -40", and show the limits in the legend.

12. **Cards are rebuilt every 2 seconds and are not accessible** (lines 273, 448-452, 483-495). `innerHTML` replaces all cards on every refresh, so the hover frame flickers and a click that starts before and ends after a refresh may be lost. Cards are plain `div` elements: no keyboard focus, no role. *Fix:* update values in place, or make each card a `<button>`; add `role="tablist"` / `aria-selected` to the tabs.

13. **Error banners do not tell the viewer what to do** (lines 454-455, 493). "The server does not answer. Showing the last data received." has no time of the last data. "Indexer unreachable: <raw error text>" shows a technical message. *Fix:* "The page cannot reach its server (last data 12:03:10 UTC). Is the ssh tunnel window still open?" and "The indexer (Kupo) is not answering; data may be old." with the raw error only on the Details tab.

14. **Empty states give no next step** (lines 273, 290, 314). "No sensor readings on the chain yet.", "No gateways known.", "No batches on the chain yet." Before the first answer the header shows "-" everywhere. *Fix:* "Nothing yet. Data appears about 10 seconds after the sensors start." and a "loading..." state for the first 2 seconds.

15. **The same idea has different names in different places.** The age of a batch is "Arrived" (Trust), "Seen" (Details), "Last data" and "Last batch seen" (gateway tables); the gateway is "Gateway", "Name", "Gateway (signer)" and "unknown signer"; a card says "updated 12 s ago" for the time the server saw the reading, not the sensor's own time (line 269). *Fix:* use one word for each idea ("Seen on chain", "Gateway") and explain "unknown signer" once: "a wallet that is not in `gateways.json`".
